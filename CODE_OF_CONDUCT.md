# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not people. Don't be a jerk. Assume good faith first; ask before escalating.

## Specifics

Harassment, personal attacks, derogatory comments, dismissive behavior toward newcomers, and any conduct that would reasonably make a contributor feel unwelcome are not acceptable.

Discussion of personal-AI systems naturally touches on personal data, health, finance, and other sensitive topics. Treat any details others share — even in public issues — with discretion. If you're unsure whether something is appropriate to share publicly, don't.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer. Privacy of the reporter is respected.

Consequences scale with severity: a private clarification for first-time low-severity issues; a public warning for repeated or moderate issues; a temporary or permanent ban for severe or sustained issues.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, any official communication channels. It also applies to maintainer-on-contributor and contributor-on-contributor interactions.
